# Competitor offer walls: how existing products present partner offers / perks / coupons (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-14. Evidence grading used throughout:
- **[F]** = I fetched and read the page itself.
- **[S]** = taken from a search-result summary/snippet of that page (page not opened, or it returned 403/404). Treat wording as approximate.
- Anything in an "Inferences" block is my reasoning, not sourced.

Important limitation up front: almost no source describes the *pixel-level* card anatomy (logo tile treatment, exact badge placement). Help centres and trade press describe flows and labels, not visuals. Mobbin / Pageflows / growth.design teardowns of these specific screens were not found in the searches run. Logo-handling findings are therefore mostly in Gaps/Inferences.

## 1. Per-category product descriptions: what does each offer wall look like and how is it organised?

### Takeaway
Across all four categories the wall is a list/grid of merchant-led tiles with one value headline and one primary action; the differences are in what the action does (add to card, reveal code, redeem at venue, start task). The closest analogues to Vibe City are not the big ticketing apps (which sell paid add-ons, not partner perks) but festival "wristband discount" lists, destination passes (Bandwango) and The Entertainer.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Event / ticketing perks**
- DICE's nearest feature is "DICE Extras", an in-app marketplace where event creators sell auxiliary products directly to fans; it is a paid upsell, not a free partner-perk wall. — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/dice-launches-dice-extras-tool-as-part-of-new-in-app-marketplace-for-event-creators-to-sell-auxiliary-products-directly-to-fans) [S]
- Eventbrite positions add-ons as merch, VIP upgrades or drink vouchers sold with the ticket. — [Eventbrite blog](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/solutions/ticket-add-ons/) [S]
- Fever's partner docs recommend gating an add-on so it can only be bought with a specific ticket type, and say an easier experience lifts conversion. — [Fever partner help](https://feverpartners.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/36078842908306-How-to-Create-Add-ons) [S]
- No official DICE, Eventbrite or Fever page describing free third-party partner perks for ticket holders was found. — search of [dice.fm](https://dice.fm/) and trade press [S]
- Manchester Punk Festival 2026 "The Discounts": a plain web page, one entry per venue = bold venue name, a one-line offer ("10% off your bill", "pizza and a drink for £12", "20% off food"), then a short casual blurb. No per-venue terms, dates or caps. One universal redemption rule: show the wristband at the till. — [MPF2026: The Discounts](https://www.manchesterpunkfestival.co.uk/mpf2026-the-discounts/) [F] (page undated; image uploads dated March 2026)
- The Great Escape (Brighton) launched "TGE Wristband Rewards" for its May 2026 edition: wristband unlocks perks across the city — discounts, special offers, complimentary items with in-store spend; typical example 10% off; named partners include Shelter Hall, Chilli Pickle, Beach Box Spa; full list lives in the official TGE app. — [Brighton and Hove News, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.brightonandhovenews.org/2026/03/31/the-great-escape-2026-announces-100-new-artists/) [S]; festival's own page [greatescapefestival.com/?p=280438](https://greatescapefestival.com/?p=280438) returned 404 when fetched
- Other festivals publish the same kind of list: Sled Island "Discount Guide" — [sledisland.com/discounts](https://sledisland.com/discounts) [S]; WinterWonderGrass "LocalWonderGrass partners" — [winterwondergrass.com](https://winterwondergrass.com/localwondergrass) [S]
- Destination "Show Your Badge" passes (Pittsburgh, Omaha) run the same idea for event badge holders; Pittsburgh's runs on Bandwango, where users scroll partner businesses and tap a listing to see its offers. — [VisitPittsburgh](https://visitpittsburgh.com/meetings-and-events/show-your-badge) [S]; [Visit Omaha](https://www.visitomaha.com/meetings/meeting-planner-toolbox/visit-omaha-badge-program/) [S]
- Bandwango passes are web-based, delivered by text and email, no app download. — [G2 listing](https://www.g2.com/products/bandwango-experience-builder/discuss) [S]; [Bandwango demo pass](https://app.bandwango.com/checkout/557/dma-west/2869/demo-passes-offer) [S]
- Not every festival perk is official: Coachella-area restaurant wristband deals are individual restaurants' own promotions. — [Cactus Hugs](https://cactushugs.com/coachella-wristband-food-discounts/) [S]

**(b) Membership / loyalty perk walls**
- Amex Offers: each offer has a spend requirement and its own expiry date; the user must add the offer to a card before spending; no retroactive credit. — [Bankrate guide](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/rewards/amex-offers-guide) [S]; [CreditCards.com](https://www.creditcards.com/news/amex-offers-guide/) [S]
- Amex only displays about 100 offers at a time; adding offers frees space for others to appear. — [Upgraded Points, updated 15 Sep 2025](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Chase Offers: an "Offers Wallet" with three tabs — "Added", "Redeemed", "Expired"; account switcher ("Choose account") at top; offer detail shows the linked card image and last four digits. The FAQ describes no categories or filters. — [Chase Offers FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs) [F]
- Chase has no native "expiring soon" sort found; that exists in third-party CardPointers ("Offer End" sort). — [CardPointers help](https://help.cardpointers.com/article/57-filtering-and-sorting-offers) [S]
- The Entertainer: browse merchants, open a merchant, pick one of its offers; all casual-dining deals are 2-for-1; three offers per merchant (per merchant, not per outlet); each offer card has an "Offer Terms" link on the merchant detail screen. — [Entertainer support, modified 19 Sep 2025](https://support.theentertainerme.com/support/solutions/articles/157000368310-how-do-i-redeem-an-offer-) [F]; [help FAQ](https://theentertainerme.com/support/cb-help.html) [S]; [merchant-refusal article](https://support.theentertainerme.com/support/solutions/articles/157000368315-what-do-i-do-if-a-merchant-refuses-to-honour-my-entertainer-offer-) [S]
- Student Beans: top-level navigation is Featured / Online / In-store / iD, with search on every page, because research showed two distinct use cases (online vs in-store). The digital iD card is surfaced prominently. — [Student Beans design case study, Medium](https://medium.com/pion/case-study-designing-the-student-beans-apps-682afacb313d) [S] (date not captured; describes the original app launch, so likely several years old)
- Perkbox: perk tiles show the *saving type* before click-through; perk types are digital vouchers, discount codes, special links, and Flexi perks paid with points; a "Perks" section with "Order History" stores past codes. — [Perkbox: different ways to save](https://help.perkbox.com/en/articles/704280-different-ways-to-save-with-perks) [S]; [How to find your redemptions](https://help.perkbox.com/en/articles/321098-how-to-find-your-redemptions) [S]
- Grab (Malaysia): claimed vouchers/rebates live under "My Rewards" with a "Use Now" button. — [Grab MY blog](https://www.grab.com/my/blog/grabpay-deals-augusts-in-store-savings/) [S] (campaign pages from roughly 2021-2022; current app may differ)

**(c) Rewarded offerwalls**
- Tapjoy (now Unity) multi-reward offers: one offer card contains several staged rewards, backed by a visual progress bar showing where the user is; users see more progress information with each step. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/tapjoy-deep-multi-reward-engagements-ads-android) [S]; [Marketing Dive](https://marketingdive.com/news/huuuge-games-boosts-roas-in-test-of-tapjoy-ad-format/574638) [S]
- Unity lists Multi-reward CPE and Daily Rewards as offerwall campaign types. — [Unity Tapjoy product page](https://unity.com/products/tapjoy) [S]
- The offer icon is the first thing the user's eye is drawn to. — [Monlix](https://monlix.com/blog/monetization/4-steps-of-advertiser-user-flow-optimization) [S]
- Guidance is to match the offerwall's UI to the host app's look and feel. — [Appnext glossary](https://appnext.com/glossary/offer-wall) [S]
- Digital Turbine reports that adding an Offer Wall carousel, videos and creative galleries on offer info pages improved UX and performance (vendor claim). — [Digital Turbine](https://www.digitalturbine.com/?p=7091) [S]

**(d) Coupon marketplaces**
- RetailMeNot's classic pattern is a "show coupon code" button that reveals the code, which the user copies to checkout. — [Money Crashers review](https://www.moneycrashers.com/retailmenot-review/) [S]
- RetailMeNot relaunched as a Rewards app on 5 Aug 2026 with personalised offer feeds, AI "Smart Search", "Scout Price", guaranteed minimum 1% cash back at 3,500+ merchants, and a "Working Code Promise" (codes for participating merchants checked before being shown). — [RetailMeNot press release, 5 Aug 2026](https://retailmenot.mediaroom.com/2026-08-05-RetailMeNot-Launches-Rewards-App-Featuring-AI-Smart-Search-and-Industry-First-Working-Code-Promise) [F]
- Groupon: purchased vouchers sit in the buyer's account; merchant taps "Mark as Redeemed" or scans the barcode; customers can also mark their own vouchers redeemed. — [Groupon merchant: how to redeem vouchers](https://www.groupon.com/merchant-content/working-with-groupon/how-it-works/how-to-redeem-groupon-vouchers) [S]
- Honey is an auto-apply browser extension rather than a browsable wall; relevant mainly as a trust cautionary tale (see section 5). — [9to5Google, 3 Jan 2025](https://9to5google.com/2025/01/03/honey-paypal-chrome-extension-lost-users/) [S]

### Inferences
- Major ticketing apps treat "extras" as revenue (paid add-ons). A free partner-perk wall unlocked by a ticket is essentially unoccupied by them; the working precedents are festival wristband programs and tourism passes, which are low-tech (a web page or a web pass).
- The festival precedents are venue-first (venue name is the heading, offer is the sub-line). Card-linked walls are also merchant-first. Nobody found leads with the category.
- Student Beans' online/in-store split is the best-documented information-architecture decision; for Vibe City, where everything is in-venue, the equivalent first-level split would more likely be by venue type (bar / cafe / wellness).

### Gaps
- Klook: nothing found on how it presents partner offers/perks to users; results were all B2B distribution news.
- Ticketmaster partner perks, UNiDAYS UI detail, ID.me, AdGem, ironSource UI: no usable descriptive source found (ironSource best-practices page now redirects to generic Unity docs).
- No Mobbin/Pageflows/growth.design teardown of any of these offer-wall screens surfaced.

## 2. What information appears on each offer card, and how are brand logos handled?

### Takeaway
Documented card contents are consistently: merchant identity, one value headline, an expiry or validity cue, a single add/claim control, and a link to terms. "Remaining count" on cards is not documented for any product surveyed; logo treatment is essentially undocumented in text sources.

### Cited Findings
- Amex Offers tile (web, 2025 layout): a "+" control beside each offer (replacing an "Add to Card" button), a "View Details" button opening a small pop-up overview, and inside it a "View Offer Terms" link. — [Upgraded Points, 15 Sep 2025](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Amex offers carry a spend threshold and an individual expiry date, typically 1-3 months out; expired offers simply disappear. — [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/rewards/amex-offers-guide) [S]; [Prince of Travel](https://princeoftravel.com/guides/amex-offers/) [S]
- Amex caps the number of card members who can redeem select offers (a hidden, not displayed, quantity limit). — [The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/how-to-get-more-amex-offers/) [S]
- Chase: offer detail shows which card it is linked to (card image + last four digits). — [Chase FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs) [F]
- Entertainer: each offer card exposes "Offer Terms". — [Entertainer support](https://support.theentertainerme.com/support/solutions/articles/157000368315-what-do-i-do-if-a-merchant-refuses-to-honour-my-entertainer-offer-) [S]
- Perkbox tiles show the saving type (voucher / code / link) before the user clicks. — [Perkbox help](https://help.perkbox.com/en/articles/704280-different-ways-to-save-with-perks) [S]
- Festival list entry: venue name, one-line offer, flavour blurb; nothing else. — [MPF2026](https://www.manchesterpunkfestival.co.uk/mpf2026-the-discounts/) [F]
- Offerwall card: icon first, then brief requirement and reward; guidance says state requirements and rewards briefly and use icons and progress bars. — [Monlix](https://monlix.com/blog/monetization/4-steps-of-advertiser-user-flow-optimization) [S]; [AdBreak Media, 18 May 2026](https://adbreakmedia.com/blog/top-user-complaints-offerwalls-fixes) [F]
- Unity suggests testing animated GIF vs still image for the offer icon. — [Unity blog](https://unity.com/pt/blog/how-to-optimize-user-flow-conversion-to-level-up-your-offerwall-performance) [S]
- CTA wording documented: "Add to Card" / "+" (Amex); "Get Discount" (Student Beans, generates a unique one-time code); "show coupon code" (RetailMeNot); "Use Now" (Grab); "Redeem" and "Check-In" (Bandwango); "Claim Now" (Bandwango prizes); "Mark as Redeemed" (Groupon merchant side). — [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]; [Student Beans help](https://help.studentbeans.com/hc/en-us/articles/25292291794716-How-to-use-Student-Beans-Online-vs-In-store) [S]; [Money Crashers](https://www.moneycrashers.com/retailmenot-review/) [S]; [Grab MY](https://www.grab.com/my/blog/grabpay-deals-augusts-in-store-savings/) [S]; [Bandwango help](https://help.bandwango.com/en/articles/11201556-redemption-methods-for-coupons-vouchers-activities) [S]; [Bandwango points](https://www.bandwango.com/resources/how-bandwangos-points-system-works) [S]; [Groupon merchant](https://www.groupon.com/merchant-content/working-with-groupon/how-it-works/how-to-redeem-groupon-vouchers) [S]

### Inferences
- From general familiarity with these products (NOT verified in this research): card-linked walls and student-discount apps normally place the merchant logo on a uniform neutral/white tile so mixed-quality logos look consistent, while venue-led products (Entertainer, Groupon, Klook, Fever) lead with a photo and overlay or append the logo; offerwalls use the advertiser's square app icon. Treat this as a hypothesis to check against live screenshots before it goes in the brief.
- None of the surveyed walls was documented as showing a visible "X left" counter. Where caps exist (Amex) they are invisible and the user discovers them by missing out. A visible, truthful remaining count would be a differentiator, and it stays on the right side of the regulators only if the number is real (see section 5).
- The minimum viable card, common to every product: who, what you get, until when, one button, terms one tap away.

### Gaps
- Logo treatment (white tile vs brand-colour tile vs photo) for every product: not described in any source found. Needs direct screenshot review (App Store screenshots, Mobbin).
- Whether any product shows remaining-quantity on the card: not found.
- Category label/badge placement on cards: not found.

## 3. How is claiming done, and what state changes does the card show afterwards?

### Takeaway
Three claim models exist: (1) one-tap save with no proof needed at the venue (card-linked), (2) reveal a code or show an ID/coupon screen, (3) venue-validated redemption (merchant PIN, QR scan, GPS check-in). Post-claim state is shown by turning the control into a check/"Added"/greyed button and moving the offer into a separate wallet list.

### Cited Findings
- Amex: tapping "+" adds the offer without a page reload; the old "Add to Card" button reloaded the page on every click and lagged or failed when adding several offers quickly. — [Upgraded Points, 15 Sep 2025](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Chase: on web the plus sign changes to a check mark; the mobile app shows "ADDED"; older guidance describes a green check in the tile's top-right corner. — [FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/chase-offers) [S]; [The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/new-chase-offers-program) [S] (which of the two sources carries which wording was not verified)
- Chase lifecycle states: Added -> Redeemed or Expired, each its own tab; Redeemed and Expired are kept for 90 days; an added offer cannot be removed; no limit on how many can be added; an offer can show as expired while the qualifying payment is still pending. — [Chase FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs) [F]
- Entertainer: at bill time tell the server, tap the offer, merchant enters a 4-digit PIN; a "Redemption Code" is displayed on success, which the merchant may note; repeat for each extra offer. — [Entertainer support, 19 Sep 2025](https://support.theentertainerme.com/support/solutions/articles/157000368310-how-do-i-redeem-an-offer-) [F]
- Entertainer: redemption can be cancelled until the merchant PIN is entered; after that it cannot be reversed. Once a merchant's three offers are used, all its locations lock. — [Entertainer FAQ](https://theentertainerme.com/support/cb-help.html) [S]
- Student Beans online: "Get Discount" generates a unique one-time-use code to paste at checkout. In-store: open the iD wallet (app only) and show it; some stores scan a QR from the app. — [Student Beans help](https://help.studentbeans.com/hc/en-us/articles/25292291794716-How-to-use-Student-Beans-Online-vs-In-store) [S] (fetch returned 403)
- UNiDAYS in-store: show the UNiDAYS iD at checkout; some retailers accept it online only. — [Holland & Barrett help](https://hollandandbarretthelp1727363657.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/27847831122194) [S]
- Grab: open "My Rewards", tap "Use Now", show the voucher to the cashier, then pay with GrabPay; some offers auto-apply with no tap; rebates must be activated via "Use Now" within 30 days of issue. — [Grab MY](https://www.grab.com/my/blog/grabpay-deals-augusts-in-store-savings/) [S] (2021-22 era)
- Bandwango ungated: find location -> "Redeem" -> select the discount -> "redeem" -> pass generates a coupon to show at checkout. — [Cache Pass help sheet](https://poudreheritage.org/wp-content/uploads/Cache-Pass-Help-Sheets.pdf) [S]; [Bandwango help](https://help.bandwango.com/en/articles/11201556-redemption-methods-for-coupons-vouchers-activities) [S]
- Bandwango gated options the organiser can choose: venue 4-digit PIN, device GPS, GPS-or-PIN (fallback for poor signal), or QR scan. After a GPS check-in the "Check-In" button greys out. — [Bandwango help](https://help.bandwango.com/en/articles/11201556-redemption-methods-for-coupons-vouchers-activities) [S]; [City of Hurst instructions](https://www.hursttx.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/14476/639116758112700000) [S]
- Groupon: scanning the barcode in the merchant app marks the voucher redeemed automatically; customers can self-mark as redeemed; merchants are told to check voucher status first because of refunds. — [Groupon merchant](https://www.groupon.com/merchant-content/working-with-groupon/how-it-works/how-to-redeem-groupon-vouchers) [S]
- Perkbox: redeemed codes/gift-card numbers are retrievable under Perks -> Order History. — [Perkbox help](https://help.perkbox.com/en/articles/321098-how-to-find-your-redemptions) [S]
- Festival wristband programs: no claim step at all; show the wristband at the till. — [MPF2026](https://www.manchesterpunkfestival.co.uk/mpf2026-the-discounts/) [F]
- Tapjoy multi-reward: state is a progress bar across staged rewards. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/tapjoy-deep-multi-reward-engagements-ads-android) [S]

### Inferences
- Two distinct moments are separated in the mature products: *claim/save* (reserves or bookmarks, changes card to "Added") and *redeem* (consumed at the venue, irreversible). Chase's Added / Redeemed / Expired is the cleanest vocabulary for a three-state card. For capped Vibe City offers the same split maps to claim (decrements the remaining count) then redeem at venue.
- Entertainer's "cancel until the merchant PIN is entered" is a useful guard against accidental redemption; the PIN/QR/GPS-or-PIN options in Bandwango show that a venue PIN is the lowest-tech validated method and survives bad connectivity.
- Wristband-flash has zero friction but gives the organiser no redemption data and cannot enforce a cap.

### Gaps
- Exact post-redemption screen design (colours, timers, animated anti-screenshot elements) for Entertainer, Grab, Student Beans: not documented in sources found.
- Whether any product lets a user "unclaim" to release a capped slot: not found (Chase explicitly does not allow removal).

## 4. What sorting / filtering / featured mechanisms exist?

### Takeaway
Documented organisation is thin and simple: a featured/curated first tab, a split by redemption context, status tabs for the user's own offers, and location-based listing. Native "ending soon" or "popular" sorts were not confirmed for any product.

### Cited Findings
- Student Beans: tabs Featured / Online / In-store / iD, search on all pages. — [Student Beans case study](https://medium.com/pion/case-study-designing-the-student-beans-apps-682afacb313d) [S]
- Chase: status tabs Added / Redeemed / Expired plus account switcher; no category filters documented; offers are personalised per card based on usage. — [Chase FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs) [F]
- "Expiring soonest" sort for card offers exists in the third-party tool CardPointers ("Offer End"), implying demand the native apps did not meet at the time. — [CardPointers help](https://help.cardpointers.com/article/57-filtering-and-sorting-offers) [S]
- Amex shows a limited window of ~100 offers. — [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Offerwall guidance: group offers into categories, highlight popular or high-value options, personalise, rotate out stale offers. — [AdBreak Media, 18 May 2026](https://adbreakmedia.com/blog/top-user-complaints-offerwalls-fixes) [F]
- Digital Turbine added a featured carousel to its Offer Wall (vendor-reported improvement). — [Digital Turbine](https://www.digitalturbine.com/?p=7091) [S]
- RetailMeNot 2026 app: personalised feeds plus conversational AI search rather than manual filtering. — [RetailMeNot press release, 5 Aug 2026](https://retailmenot.mediaroom.com/2026-08-05-RetailMeNot-Launches-Rewards-App-Featuring-AI-Smart-Search-and-Industry-First-Working-Code-Promise) [F]
- Bandwango: scroll a list of locations and open each listing. — [VisitPittsburgh](https://visitpittsburgh.com/meetings-and-events/show-your-badge) [S]
- Entertainer's App Store listing mentions improved search, savings analytics and preference settings but does not describe filters. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-entertainer/id702813714) [S]
- Festival lists: unsorted flat list of venues. — [MPF2026](https://www.manchesterpunkfestival.co.uk/mpf2026-the-discounts/) [F]

### Inferences
- With a small catalogue (a festival list is typically a dozen or so venues), products do not bother with filters at all. Filters appear when catalogues reach hundreds of offers. For a small local wall, a featured slot plus 3-4 category chips is likely sufficient; heavier filtering would be over-building.
- A "My offers" view split by status (claimed / used / expired) is the one organising device that appears even where no browse filters exist.

### Gaps
- Native "near me", "ending soon", "popular/trending" sorts in Entertainer, Grab, Klook, UNiDAYS, Amex: widely believed to exist but not confirmed by any source found here.
- How "featured" slots are chosen (paid vs editorial) and whether they are labelled as sponsored: not found.

## 5. What do reviews, teardowns and regulators say works or annoys?

### Takeaway
The dominant complaints are about broken promises, not layout: offers that do not work at the point of use (expired codes, merchants refusing, vouchers wrongly shown as used), unclear expiry, and missing rewards. Fake urgency is a documented regulatory risk. What is praised is speed of adding and responsive support.

### Cited Findings
- Offerwall complaints (trade blog, no statistics cited): cluttered/confusing layouts causing abandonment; delayed or missing rewards ("erodes trust faster" than anything); high volume of low-value, irrelevant offers; intrusive pop-ups and notifications. Fixes: simple layout, categories, highlight high-value, instant or clearly timed rewards, easy support channel, prune stale offers, opt-in notifications. — [AdBreak Media, 18 May 2026](https://adbreakmedia.com/blog/top-user-complaints-offerwalls-fixes) [F] (vendor blog, not user research)
- Amex: full page reload on every "Add to Card" was a known friction; replaced by an inline "+". The article's "faster, cleaner" verdict is the author's view, no user data. — [Upgraded Points, 15 Sep 2025](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Amex: forgetting to add before purchase means no credit, with no retroactive fix. — [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/credit-cards/rewards/amex-offers-guide) [S]
- Entertainer: App Store reviewers report restaurants refusing the app (e.g. "contract was over"), and Trustpilot reviewers say venues have been removed over the years and vendors interpret terms their own way. App Store rating about 4.8 from ~46,000 ratings versus Trustpilot 1.8 from 49 reviews (small sample). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-entertainer/id702813714) [S]; [Trustpilot](https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/www.theentertainerme.com) [S]
- Perkbox: complaints about codes the vendor did not recognise, gift cards not working, app crashes causing duplicate purchases, an expiry policy a reviewer called misleading (Perkbox replied the date is shown correctly but it wants to make it clearer), and poor VoiceOver support. Support responsiveness is frequently praised. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/perkbox.com?page=2) [S]; [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/perkbox/id1297386760) [S]
- Perkbox summary cons per a review aggregator: limited discount value, technical issues, complex redemption. — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/perkbox/reviews) [S]
- RetailMeNot admits community-submitted codes may be expired or invalid; its 2026 "Working Code Promise" is positioned as the answer to failed codes at checkout. — [Trustpilot reply](https://it.trustpilot.com/review/www.community.retailmenot.com) [S]; [press release, 5 Aug 2026](https://retailmenot.mediaroom.com/2026-08-05-RetailMeNot-Launches-Rewards-App-Featuring-AI-Smart-Search-and-Industry-First-Working-Code-Promise) [F]
- A coupon-industry survey reports 35% of respondents have had a code fail because it was expired (most common failure type). Methodology unverified; publisher is a coupon company. — [SimplyCodes](https://simplycodes.com/blog/coupon-statistics-2026) [S]
- Groupon: vouchers shown as redeemed when the customer says they were not; vouchers stay in the account until the user marks them used, making it hard to tell identical vouchers apart. — [Groupon FAQ](https://www.groupon.co.uk/faq/using-your-groupon/troubleshooting-groupon-voucher-redemption-issues) [S]; [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/groupon.co.uk?page=7) [S]; [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/online/groupon.html) [S]
- Honey: after a Dec 2024 exposé alleging it claimed affiliate credit even when no coupon applied, it lost about 3 million of 20 million Chrome users within two weeks and was later reported at 14 million (July 2025). Figures differ between reports. — [9to5Google, 3 Jan 2025](https://9to5google.com/2025/01/03/honey-paypal-chrome-extension-lost-users/) [S]; [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/honey-drops-to-14-million-chrome-users-amid-ongoing-affiliate-scandal/) [S]
- Fake urgency: a Princeton/University of Chicago crawl of about 11,000 shopping sites found 157 deceptive countdown timers on 140 sites and 1,800+ dark-pattern instances overall. — [Quartz summary](https://things.qz.com/2019/dark-patterns/index.html?c=28) [S]
- The FTC's 2022 staff report lists baseless countdown timers and false low-stock messages as deceptive. — [Sheppard Mullin](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/ftc-reports-rise-in-dark-pattern-tactics-in-consumer-markets) [S]
- The UK CMA found Emma Sleep's timers misleading because the timer's end did not reflect real availability of the discount. — [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/it-s-final-countdown-or-it-cma-issues-decision-online-pressure-selling-tactics) [S]; [Osborne Clarke](https://marketinglaw.osborneclarke.com/advertising-regulation/under-pressure-cma-calls-on-emma-sleep-to-change-its-online-selling-practices/) [S]
- Singapore regulators have also flagged online retailers for dark patterns (regional signal; details not read). — [Pulse summary](https://www.pulse.bot/ecommerce/news/dark-patterns-singapore-online-retailers-caught-misleading-shoppers-2edf4796-4b37-48a0-bb73-0dd9ce011876/) [S]

### Inferences
- The failure that hurts most happens at the venue counter, not in the app: staff who do not know the offer, or an offer that has lapsed. For a partner wall this argues for venue-side briefing and for removing or clearly disabling ended offers rather than leaving them browsable.
- Scarcity and countdowns are safe only when literally true. Genuinely limited claims with a real counter are legitimate; resetting timers or invented stock levels are what regulators penalise.
- Store a visible history of what was claimed/used (Chase tabs, Perkbox Order History); its absence is what the Groupon complaints describe.

### Gaps
- No experiment or survey measuring how fake urgency changes trust in deals was found.
- No Reddit threads were retrieved; complaints come from Trustpilot/App Store/ConsumerAffairs snippets and are anecdotal.
- No Philippine-specific regulator guidance on urgency claims was searched for or found.
- No independent teardown of GrabRewards, Klook or Entertainer UX found.

## 6. Positioning: how do products describe the offers to users?

### Takeaway
Tone tracks the business model: banks use neutral, transactional language (offers, add, statement credit); membership apps sell volume and savings; festival programs use warm, local, in-group voice and frame offers as a reward for belonging.

### Cited Findings
- Chase vocabulary: "Chase Offers", "Offers Wallet", "Added", "Redeemed", "Expired", "statement credits". — [Chase FAQ](https://www.chase.com/personal/merchant-offers/faqs) [F]
- Amex vocabulary: "Amex Offers", "Add to Card", "View Details", "View Offer Terms". — [Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-offers-layout-change/) [F]
- Entertainer App Store headline: "1000's of offers in your city"; core promise is 2-for-1. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/kw/app/the-entertainer/id702813714) [S]
- The Great Escape names its program "Wristband Rewards" and describes "exclusive perks across the city". — [Brighton and Hove News, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.brightonandhovenews.org/2026/03/31/the-great-escape-2026-announces-100-new-artists/) [S]
- Manchester Punk Festival: headline "The Discounts"; informal, slangy blurbs per venue ("The best coffee shop in Manchester"; "Just show your MPF wristband at the till, sorted"); sign-off "See you in the pit". — [MPF2026](https://www.manchesterpunkfestival.co.uk/mpf2026-the-discounts/) [F]
- Destination passes use an instruction as the name: "Show Your Badge", "Show us your wristband & save". — [VisitPittsburgh](https://visitpittsburgh.com/meetings-and-events/show-your-badge) [S]; [Go To Louisville](https://www.gotolouisville.com/discounts/show-us-your-wristband-save-2) [S]
- RetailMeNot 2026 repositioning: from "a savings destination" to "an intelligent, personalized shopping platform"; trust claim "Working Code Promise". — [press release, 5 Aug 2026](https://retailmenot.mediaroom.com/2026-08-05-RetailMeNot-Launches-Rewards-App-Featuring-AI-Smart-Search-and-Industry-First-Working-Code-Promise) [F]
- Offerwall copy guidance: reword demand-style task text into benefit-led wording; keep steps short because users do not read long. — [Digital Turbine developer guidance](https://developer.digitalturbine.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/17831809099676) [S]
- Student Beans frames the digital iD as replacing the physical student card. — [Student Beans case study](https://medium.com/pion/case-study-designing-the-student-beans-apps-682afacb313d) [S]

### Inferences
- The festival programs that resemble Vibe City most do not say "coupons" or "deals"; they say rewards/perks/discounts tied to the wristband and write each venue up like a recommendation from a friend. The offer reads as hospitality from the neighbourhood rather than advertising.
- Naming the program after the proof of entitlement ("Wristband Rewards", "Show Your Badge") doubles as the redemption instruction.
- Trust copy is becoming a headline feature in the coupon category (RetailMeNot's promise), suggesting "every offer here actually works" is a positioning asset, not just hygiene.
